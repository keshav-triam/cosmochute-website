The thesis

Fifty years of lunar exploration produced brilliant landings and dead hardware. Every mission carried its own everything, used it once, and left it. The economics of that model don't close — not for science, not for industry.

Our answer is boring on purpose: infrastructure. A rover that outlives any single payload. A trailer that carries the next eight jobs. A campaign, not a stunt.

The program

LEAP is one landing that becomes many missions. A lander delivers the convoy; EPOC — the rover — tows OASys, a magazine of standardized 8U payload cartridges. At each work site the arm swaps a cartridge into EPOC's bay, the payload operates, data goes home via relay, and the convoy rolls onward. Crater to crater. Never back.

The principles

Reusable or wrong. Hardware that can't be reassigned is cargo, not infrastructure.

Survive the night. Two weeks of −180°C is the entry fee. We pay it in thermal design, not hope.

Standard interfaces. The 8U cartridge means your payload doesn't need to be a spacecraft — it needs to fit a box.

ABOUT — PROGRAM TIMELINE

FROM A QUESTIONTO A CAMPAIGN.

Every piece of the fleet answers one question we couldn't let go of: why does everything we send to the Moon get used exactly once?

T-00 · THE QUESTION

One landing, one job, one grave

We mapped fifty years of surface missions and found the same silhouette: brilliant machines, single use, abandoned. The Moon is a museum of hardware that worked.

T-01 · THE STANDARD

The 8U cartridge

Before any vehicle, we fixed the interface: a standardized payload box with power, data and thermal on one face. If payloads are cartridges, the vehicle becomes a platform.

T-02 · THE VEHICLE

EPOC

A six-wheel, rocker-bogie rover with a payload bay and a manipulation arm. Built to be refuelled by sunlight, survive the night, and never meet a payload it can't host.

T-03 · THE CONVOY

OASys

The reload trailer: an eight-slot magazine on wheels. Hitched behind EPOC, it turns one traverse into eight missions. The trailer goes everywhere — except to the next landing site.

T-04 · THE CAMPAIGN

LEAP

Land once. Egress. Work crater to crater, swapping cartridges at each site, relaying data home. Park the trailer at the edge of the worked zone and drive on to meet the next lander.

T-05 · ONWARD

Infrastructure, compounding

Each campaign leaves behind charged, parked, reusable capacity. The second landing starts richer than the first. That's the whole company in one sentence.
