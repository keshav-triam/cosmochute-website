PARTNERS — THE OFFER

YOUR PAYLOAD.OUR CONVOY.

Put an instrument on the lunar surface without building a spacecraft. If it fits an 8U cartridge, it rides the magazine — and the fleet does the rest.

FIVE LINKS FROM IDEA TO DATA

Fit the box

Design to the 8U cartridge standard — power, data and thermal on one face. We supply the spec, the mass budget and an engineering contact who answers email.

Integrate & qualify

Your cartridge meets our bench before it meets the Moon: vibration, thermal-vac and interface checkout alongside our own hardware.

Ride the manifest

Cartridges load into OASys — eight slots per campaign. The lander delivers the whole convoy in one landing.

Operate on site

EPOC swaps your cartridge into its bay at your target site. Your payload gets position, power and a relay window — you get a console view.

Data home, hardware onward

Results downlink through the relay while the convoy rolls to the next site. Your slot can be re-manifested on the next campaign — that's the point.
