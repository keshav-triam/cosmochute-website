CAREERS — MISSION BRIEFING

BUILD THE MACHINESTHAT REFUSE TO DIEON THE MOON.

Most aerospace careers end at the launch pad. Ours begin at touchdown. We hire people who want their hardware to survive the 354-hour night — and then get back to work.

HOW WE WORK
01

Hardware is the résumé

Titles matter less than the machine in front of you. Everyone here can point at a part of the fleet and say: mine.

02

Survive first, impress later

The Moon punishes cleverness that isn't robust. We design for the worst orbit, the coldest night, the dustiest seal.

03

Small crew, full stakes

You will own systems end-to-end — design, test, fly. There is nowhere to hide, which is exactly the appeal.

04

Reusable people, too

Missions are marathons. We staff so nobody burns up on entry: sane hours, honest reviews, long careers.
